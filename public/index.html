VRO Converter

Convert DVD-VR .VRO recordings to MP4 and other formats. Runs on this computer — files are never uploaded anywhere else.

    On a DVD-RAM/-RW disc or camcorder, everything is in the DVD_RTAV folder: VR_MOVIE.VRO holds the video, VR_MANGR.IFO says where each recording starts and when it was filmed. Settings apply to files added after you change them.
